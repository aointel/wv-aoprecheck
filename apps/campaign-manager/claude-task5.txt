Redesign the Call Examination panel in src/components/MyScore.tsx (specifically the ScorecardView component and its sub-components FakeCallPlayer and ConversationTracks). This is for an insurance company doing outbound veteran benefit appointment-setting calls. The call has 3 speakers: AI Agent (Taalk bot), Human Agent (the rep), and Client. The goal is ONLY to get a live transfer or booked appointment — NOT a full enterprise sales cycle.

## Changes to ConversationTracks component:
Change from 2 lanes (Agent/Client) to 3 lanes:
- **AI Agent Lane** — color: rgba(99,102,241,0.7) (indigo/purple)
- **Human Agent Lane** — color: rgba(34,197,94,0.7) (green)  
- **Client Lane** — color: rgba(251,191,36,0.7) (amber)

Add a small legend below the lanes showing which color = which speaker.

Change the "AI Chapter Tracks" section from 6 enterprise chapters (Intro/Discovery/Objections/Pricing/Next Steps/Close) to 5 appointment-setting chapters:
- **Hook** (did AI/human state reason for call and grab attention?)
- **Qualify** (did they confirm veteran status before pitching?)
- **Bridge** (did they attempt live transfer/presentation first?)
- **Objection** (did they handle "not government" or other pushback?)
- **Close** (did they get hard commitment — specific date/time or live now?)
