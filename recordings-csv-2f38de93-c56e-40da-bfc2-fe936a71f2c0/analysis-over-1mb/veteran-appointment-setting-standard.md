# Veteran Appointment-Setting Standard

## Script

### Opening
- "Hello? Hey [Name]. Yeah. Hey, this is Alex with AO Globe Life Customer Service. I was calling about the Veteran Will Kit that you just requested. Can you hear me okay?"

### Agenda/Consent
- "The reason why I'm calling is that your kit has been processed and your local representative is standing by."
- "So, it looks like you had requested your Will Kit online through Facebook or Google. Does that ring a bell?"

### Qualification
- "Have you ever set up something like this before? Or is this your first time getting these kinds of documents in place?"
- "Are you married, widowed, single, or divorced? What's your spouse's name? How old are you, sir?"

### Close
- "I could do it at [time] your time, or I could take care of it now. Which would be better?"
- "So, we can do [time] on [day]. Does that work for both you and your spouse?"

### Next Steps
- "I will send you the form to request your DD-214 and ensure you know how to pre-register for your VA burial benefits."
- "You'll get some reminder text messages and emails along the way."

## Objection Handling Playbook

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have everything set up to be cremated at a local funeral home here." | "Great. Now, we can help you get established so that you... you and your wife can be laid to rest in any of the VA national cemeteries at no cost in your state." |
| "I'm not that technically proficient, so you're going to have to bear with me a little bit." | "Oh, no, no. My job is to be patient and bear with you a little bit. Okay. Maybe even a lot of it." |
| "I don't really plan on going to the cemetery or anything like that." | "Doing what you want with the ashes can be illegal about 90% of the time. It's not that much, right?" |
| "We don't have computers." | "Well, you have smartphones, right? That works just fine." |
| "I did talk to somebody once, but there was a thousand pieces of paper to sign and I said no because I just want something simple." | "You'd be surprised how many people are in the same boat. A lot of veterans assume they'll just figure it out later, but the reality is that getting these documents in order now makes things so much easier for your loved ones down the road." |
| "I can't hear you." | "Okay, let me see if I can fix that. Can you hear me a little bit better now?" |
| "I think it'd be better if you called before you came." | "Well, we don't go to your home anymore. I can call you before your appointment just to help lock you into the virtual office for veterans." |
| "I don't have access to my email." | "Okay, so [email] doesn't work for you anymore, okay? I'm going to try to make it work, but not right now." |

## Key Phrases
- "Your kit has been processed."
- "This program is set up so when something happens to you, your family will have the necessary documents to carry out your wishes."
- "We're not going to tell a veteran they have to do anything."
- "My job is to be patient and bear with you a little bit."
- "Getting these documents in order now makes things so much easier for your loved ones down the road."
- "A lot of veterans assume they'll just figure it out later, but the reality is that getting these documents in order now makes things so much easier for your loved ones down the road."
- "I understand, it can be quite frustrating when technology doesn't cooperate."
- "We do work by appointment basis only because there's so many veterans to see."
- "If you know something is going to conflict, let me know so I can maybe see another veteran during that time spot."
- "Can you hear me okay?"
- "Does that ring a bell?"
- "Are you familiar with Zoom?"
- "I'll walk you right through it so you know how to use it with the VA if you need."
- "You'll get some reminder text messages and emails along the way."
