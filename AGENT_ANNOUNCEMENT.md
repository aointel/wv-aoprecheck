# 🚀 What's New - January 2025

## Call Connector Pro Updates

### ✨ **Subscription Access Changes**
- Only agents with **active** Stripe subscriptions now have access to Call Connector Pro
- All agents without access will see an introductory video about Call Connector Pro
- **Globe Market agents** can now subscribe directly - look for the sign-up button below the video!

### 💳 **New Billing Portal**
- Manage all your Stripe subscriptions from the billing dashboard
- Change payment methods, update billing info, and view your billing history
- All "Buy Credits" buttons now take you to the same convenient billing page

## Call Metrics Improvements

### 📊 **More Accurate Statistics**
- Fixed dial counting issues - your numbers are now more accurate
- Statistics totals now reflect ALL agents, not just what's on your current page
- Instant presentations are now properly tracked and displayed

### 🤖 **Answering Machine Detection**
- Outbound calls now detect if they were answered by a human or machine
- This data is stored in your call logs for better analysis

## User Experience

### 🔔 **Fewer Notifications**
- Fixed annoying toast notifications that appeared after every call
- Your screen stays cleaner while you work

### 🎨 **Better Navigation**
- All credit purchase buttons now go to the same place for consistency
- Easier to find what you need
